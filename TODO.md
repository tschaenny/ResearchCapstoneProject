# Questions for the Botswana National Museum
 
## Priority questions for the first meeting
 
1. Who will run and update the website after 1 November? Is there an IT department (museum or government) that will host it?
2. Are there government rules for websites, e.g. a .gov.bw domain, hosting in Botswana, approved technologies or a security review?
3. How are objects recorded today: paper register, Excel or collection software? Could we get a sample export?
4. Which fields does an object record have, and what does the inventory number look like?
5. Which objects or details must not be public? For example sacred or culturally sensitive objects, human remains, value or storage location, or objects that need community consent.
6. Is admission free or paid? Which visitor categories and prices apply (citizens, residents, international visitors, children, students, schools)?
7. How many visitors can be inside at the same time? Should time slots be hourly or every 30 minutes?
8. Which gallery and which objects should we use for the QR code pilot?
9. Is there Wi-Fi or mobile coverage in the galleries?
10. Should the Website be in English with a few tswana word or toggle to switch between English and Setswana (multiple languages)
## Operations and handover
 
- Do you already have a domain, a logo, brand guidelines or social media accounts we should use?
- Who is our main contact, who approves design and content, and how often can we meet?
## Collection and database
 
- How many objects are there in total, and which 30 to 50 should go online first?
- Do photos exist? Who owns the image rights, and who can take new photos?
- Who will enter the data, how comfortable are they with computers, and is there reliable internet in the offices?
- Which languages do you need (English, Setswana, others)? Who translates?
## Tickets and visits
 
- What are the opening hours, last entry, public holidays and closure days?
- How do school and group bookings work today?
- When do guided tours run, how many people can join, and in which languages?
- Should visitors pay online? Which payment methods (cash, card, Orange Money, MyZaka, Smega)? Does the museum have an account for this, and are there rules for government income?
- How does entry work today? Does the front desk have a device with internet to check tickets?
- Which personal data may we collect from visitors, and what do you require for data protection?
## QR codes in the galleries
 
- Can we add QR codes to the existing labels, or should we print new ones?
- What size and material should labels have, and are there conservation rules for mounting them?
- What should visitors see after scanning: short text, longer text, audio, which languages?
## Design and content
 
- What do you like or dislike about the prototype?
- Is the theme "Botswana at 60" appropriate for the homepage?
- Which existing texts can we use (history, exhibitions, visitor information)?
- Are there accessibility needs we should plan for?
## Future plans
 
- Which comes first for you: virtual tour, AR, interactive displays or digitisation?
- Are there budgets, partners or equipment for these already?
## Please send us before the meeting, if possible
 
- A sample export or photo of the current object register
- A photo of a current object label
- Current opening hours and admission prices
